# Data availability

This release selection contains exactly four original R2 aggregate CSVs under data/derived_r2_candidate/: weight_grid.csv, genetic_sd.csv, partial_rg.csv and alm_restrictions.csv. The author grants CC BY 4.0 within their licensing authority. Per-file input lineage, unchanged SHA256 and retained V5 verification are in r2/DERIVED_CSV_PROVENANCE.tsv.

The complete composite.csv, bone_global.csv and bone_pairwise.csv are excluded and archived outside the repository. Their FN/LS upstream issue is EXCLUDED_PENDING_CLARIFICATION. This selection makes no claim to have resolved source noncommercial conditions or obtained a new exception. Exclusion metadata does not contain their result records.

The four retained result families depend on ALM and regional FFM/FM covariance terms; they do not report FN/LS or heel effects. The unchanged eight-trait runner can calculate additional families with authorized inputs. Complete real S/V/I/N and RDS remain private; no reduced real matrix is redistributed.

Public sources used for the retained families are Pei adjusted ALM GCST90000025 and Pan-UKB EUR regional body composition/UK Biobank. Source attribution and terms review are in DATA_LICENSE.md and r2/UPSTREAM_LICENSE_REVIEW.tsv. No upstream records are sublicensed. The synthetic S8/V36 examples are independently generated, labelled SYNTHETIC_NOT_STUDY_DATA, and do not reproduce study numbers.

Historical v1.0.0 tables and artwork remain historical with their existing scoped grants and unchanged bytes. Study-number reproduction requires authorized frozen inputs and private reference files; public files alone demonstrate synthetic execution, not upstream/end-to-end GWAS/LDSC/GenomicSEM refitting. No raw GWAS, SNP records, LD panels, participant data, private audit logs, workbook/maps or credentials are selected for publication.
