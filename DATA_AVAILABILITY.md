# Data availability and source attribution

The repository includes **selected aggregate, model-derived research results and plotting records** generated by the study authors. The source GWAS summary statistics remain with the original studies and data catalogues cited in the manuscript, Table 1 and Supplementary Table S6a. This archive does not redistribute original SNP-level GWAS or VCF summary statistics, participant-level data, LD reference panels, complete LDSC S/V/I/N matrices, or primary GenomicSEM model objects.

The author-owned derived TSVs and original Figure 2/Figure 3 artwork listed in `LICENSE_SCOPE.md` are provided under CC BY 4.0, subject to the explicit scope and attribution terms in `DATA_LICENSE.md`. Original analysis scripts listed in `LICENSE_SCOPE.md` are separately licensed under MIT. These licences **do not** apply to any original third-party GWAS dataset.

For FN-BMD and LS-BMD, the exact ancestry and download/release provenance of the harmonized-Z files and marker-specific effective sample sizes have not been independently established from the retained records. The reported study-level sample sizes must not be confused with the constant VCF SS annotations; see the manuscript and supplementary methods for the actual source boundaries.
